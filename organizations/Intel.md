# Intel

**Inference runtimes and CPU/GPU/NPU execution tools** for local and edge deployments.

[← Organizations](../README.md#organizations)

| Project | Role | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| **OpenVINO** | Inference runtime, compilation and hardware backends for Intel devices. | [GitHub](https://github.com/openvinotoolkit/openvino) |
| **OpenVINO GenAI** | LLM/VLM generation API, continuous batching, prompt/KV caching and speculative decoding on top of OpenVINO. | [GitHub](https://github.com/openvinotoolkit/openvino.genai) |
| **IPEX-LLM** | Intel-oriented low-bit LLM inference optimizations and hardware support. | [GitHub](https://github.com/intel/ipex-llm) |
| **Neural Compressor** | Quantization/compression tooling to reduce model execution cost. | [GitHub](https://github.com/intel/neural-compressor) |

**Boundary.** Tools may cover training or general DL inference as well; inclusion is based on their concrete LLM inference applicability, not brand ownership of downstream systems.

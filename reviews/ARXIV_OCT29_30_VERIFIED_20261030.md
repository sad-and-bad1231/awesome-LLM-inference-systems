## Separate R0–R3 reproduction gates

- **R0 Artifact & parity:** verify authoritative public source/commit and runnable scripts, exact model/hardware/version/dtype, original output/quality semantics, strongest installed native and newer upstream alternative. Missing public code → **BLOCKED_AUTHOR_REPRO**, though reading admission remains valid.

- **R2 Preregister & calibrate:** only after R0/R1 PASS, one controlled contrast, same outputs/token IDs, ≥3 fresh independent A/A and paired unprofiled timing, thermal/clock/observer controls, hashes/receipts and explicit STOP.

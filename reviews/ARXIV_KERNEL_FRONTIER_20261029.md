## Evidence standard and scope

For each candidate: G0 original identity / release type; G1 inference-time relevance or narrow, declared foundational/adjacent exception; G2 distinguishing mechanism and closest competitors; G3 claimed experiments, model / hardware / workload boundaries and negative results; G4 public artifact vs unreleased implementation; G5 value per finite reading time. Speedups below are **author reported**. No Ascend 910B2 port, full implementation audit or independent G3 baseline-parity experiment was performed. All canonical papers have **one** primary home in the eight topic lists.

### Frontier editorial decision

Retain **exactly six** research frontiers, and strengthen **the existing Agentic kernel engineering** frontier with a comparative subsection for JustGoal, KDA, NVIDIA/CMU CAKE and the **different** CANNBot-CAKE project. Do not promote familiar kernel optimization or vendor porting into a separate top-level frontier. Distinguish single shape versus full shape, same-model control, optimizer search cost, hidden correctness, compiler hardware, and composed **end-to-end model** performance. The original identity of the user-mentioned ForgeKernel remains an explicit open question.

**Integrity checks expected before merge:** 10 distinct canonical URLs, no repeated Uncertainty-Aware Output Length paper, 8 per-category totals sum to 776, per-subgroup counts match actual rows, every new row retains source and output/evaluation caveats. No hardware experiment, no result replication, no codebase migration, no paper-generated JSONL.

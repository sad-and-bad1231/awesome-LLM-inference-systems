## New two-speed policy applied

- **G0/G1/G2 hard:** actual source ID/title/authors, concrete LLM inference execution, falsifiable systems mechanism, distinct from already curated work. Check arXiv/DOI aliases before adding a row.
- **G3/Evidence graded:** `KEEP_PREPRINT_EXPLORE` may rest on rigorous mechanism + **a small inspectable support anchor** (limited GPU microbench, trace, original analytical proof or transparent simulator); no requirement for unshown online E2E. `KEEP_PREPRINT_EVIDENCED` has stronger scoped experiment. `KEEP_REFERENCE` has publication and method exemplar evidence. The `EXPLORE_ADJACENT` suffix demands clear transfer to real inference execution.
- **Author claims not treated as independently verified.** Particularly: fabricated PIM/OCS/multi-chiplet speedups are **simulation**, not actual accelerator boards; a PyTorch kernel speedup is **not** vLLM Serving; trained KV/PIC/quantization is **not** bitwise lossless; robot action Success Rate is **not** Token Throughput.
- Earlier dated `HOLD/REMOVE` receipts remain **historical editorial decisions**. Their entries below are *superseded* by this new evidence-tier policy for the 31 specific source IDs; do not silently rewrite archived source findings.

## Revalidation and next quality questions

1. **Identity and duplication:** 31 added arXiv IDs were absent as canonical source IDs; matched each one to an author arXiv abstract or HTML (some inaccessible HTML relied on original abstract, explicitly `EXPLORE` where experiments not fully audited).
2. **Pilot/physical separation:** HELIX, Coffee, In-DRAM/PUD, Expert Streaming, optical OCS, HeteroPanacea and analytical provisioning **must not be quoted as directly measured integrated hardware**. The papers contain precise algorithms or simulators, but their design-space benefits are conditional.
3. **Agentic systems:** [SEIS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04646) is **whole-engine evolving code with fixed agent**, [InferenceBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20468) finds even plain budgeted hyperparameter search strong, and [KernelBench-Verified](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16241) finds reward-hacking/benchmark baseline issues. These jointly ground **RSI on inference engines as an open research question**, not an already demonstrated recursive agent.
4. **Inferred transfer to Ascend:** models tested on NVIDIA GPU, AMD MI300/MI350, Apple ANE and speculative hardware **are not performance promises for Ascend 910B2**. The reusability is in mechanism, test design and boundary conditions.
5. **No individual local execution:** author artifacts may exist; no external benchmark/test/NPU run performed by this sweep. Subsequent G4 promotion should be selective (operator/compiler agent and live KV/EP high-value cases), not a barrier to arXiv discovery.

**Editorial verdict:** Broad arXiv admissions under a stricter **truthfulness floor** and flexible **maturity-specific evidence labels**. Keep the PR Draft pending current HEAD CI/catalog validation. The dated November 9 898-paper receipts are historical checkpoints, not a competing inventory.

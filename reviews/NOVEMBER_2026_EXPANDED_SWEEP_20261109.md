## Eight mechanism classes and seven frontiers

**Canonical classes:** Kernel+3, KV+2, Decoding+3, Quantization+3, MoE+1, Runtime+0, Distributed+0, Benchmarking+0 in this *second batch*. Strong work in Distributed still requires measured cluster/transport evidence; no quotas or invented papers. Full time-span source scans were selective rather than literally exhaustive.

1. **Agent-native serving:** intent-sensitive caching, multi-agent cache traces and hybrid state, with actual workloads and session lifecycle, not an application-only agent idea.
2. **Omni-modal live serving:** VLM-specific Uni-LaSpec shows multimodal drafting cost, but does not by itself prove full-duplex audio/video streaming backpressure.
3. **Diffusion language serving:** Step-dLLM + SpecCTMC distinguish approximate sparse denoising from **exact CTMC-law** speculative simulation; measure both wall-clock and semantic quality under the correct target law.
4. **Interactive world models:** no novel real-device action-to-frame/jitter/state-persistence admission in this batch; keep existing StreamDiffusionV2 anchor.
5. **VLA embodied action loops:** VLALagBench is Frontier context, not automatically an 8-topic paper. Need real-SoC perception-to-action deadline measurements.
6. **Agentic kernel engineering:** ForgeKernel's multi-session mechanism-memory + actual Ascend results is strong new evidence; distinguish **operator best-of-budget** from code-agent generalization, hidden correctness and inference E2E.
7. **Autonomous inference engine evolution:** ServerBench (earlier batch) evaluates entire serving implementations; ForgeKernel improves kernel search process and SpecPilot modifies speculative paths. This is **not proof of self-modifying optimizer or recursive self-improvement**.

## Immediate experimental/engineering priorities

- For Ascend: ForgeKernel on **910C** is not tested on the pinned **910B2/CANN 9.1**; inspect compiler/shape/oracle compatibility. FlashOffloading's 910C/PD pathway remains a separate priority.
- For NPU kernel research: FOSCAR and LoopKV require CUDA/Hopper, Blackwell or looped-model assumptions; inspect *mechanism transfer* before promising porting speedups.
- For serving: FastKVComp and Bevy are better evidence when measured end-to-end on real transport/SSD and matched baselines; avoid replacing p99/SLO Goodput with kernel-only throughput.
- For RSI-adjacent work: preserve workloads, exact input/output oracle, search-budget cap, hidden tests and frozen benchmark versions.

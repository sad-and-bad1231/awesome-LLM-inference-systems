## D. October research signal and reproducibility priority

The **most useful mechanisms to test**, not simply papers with the largest speedup number:

1. **Distributed MoE's real barriers:** StreamEP offers concrete graph-free/per-token async work and network scheduling; reproduce A/A, graph/collective overlap and routing-skew profiles on a compatible **real multi-GPU system** before any proposal for HCCL TP4/EP Ascend. Do not confuse EP=16 L40S with TP4 on 910B2.
2. **Agent serving becomes a program contract:** HEAR and FlowMaker shift the unit of scheduling from request to **tool/LLM DAG/semantic context**. Measure end-to-end Agent Critical Path, model/tool overlap, cache-hit correctness and cross-request fairness. Model selection is not exact same-policy acceleration.
3. **Hybrid long-lived memory and allocator economics:** SGLang HiCache/Unified Memory and DeepInfer's production storage split show **KV + recurrent state + network + prefix** are joint capacity and latency constraints. Compare per-tier bytes, cache-hit value, replays, materialization and P99 under controlled arrival patterns.
4. **RL Rollout vs online Serving boundaries:** EfficientRollout and TensorHub are genuine systems advances, but their gains accrue to the **training generation loop**; do not apply them uncritically to a public request-serving architecture.
5. **Performance claims require robust observables:** PowerTrace, Carbon–SLO, multilingual energy and SieveServe demonstrate why **J/token, successful-task energy, throughput under accuracy/SLO, and real-vs-simulated network** must be reported together.

**Suggested next month:** source/code review of StreamEP and DeepInfer's async scheduling + parallelism contracts first; a strict cost-model and architecture comparison with vLLM Ascend 0.30.0 **on the correct CANN pair**; then controlled A/A baselines before optimizing. Inference-map recording can reuse the project-specific experimental evidence but should not copy author results as local measurements.

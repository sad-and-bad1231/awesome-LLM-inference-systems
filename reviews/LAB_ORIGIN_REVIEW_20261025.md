## Audit notes

- **Not venue inflation:** MORI, Foundry, FreeToken, CommBench, M*, MURMUR, VoxServe, VibeServe, TraceLab are 2026 preprints even when their authors have strong university affiliations. DuoAttention is **ICLR 2025**, while Fast-dLLM v2 is **ICLR 2026** (2025 initial preprint), RoMeo is **PPoPP 2026**, FlashFuser is **HPCA 2026**, Ekka is **ICML 2026**.
- **Model/quality changes:** DuoAttention compresses KV approximately; Fast-dLLM v2 requires retraining into a different diffusion model; RoMeo uses quantized computation; MURMUR accepts an accuracy–latency frontier. None are presented as drop-in bitwise output-equivalent replacements for the frozen vLLM v0.23/Ascend 910B2 baseline.
- **Source hierarchy:** paper/venue + author repository and evaluation; all speedup values attributed to authors, exact compute conditions scoped in canonical entries. Unreleased MORI patches and code gaps remain explicit.
- **Duplicate and count invariant:** one canonical URL/title across eight mechanism pages; QServe remains only on historical homepage. Topic totals must sum to 736; the corpus is not marked independently reproduced.

## Requested paper: already canonical, improved

**[IntAttention: A Fully Integer Attention Pipeline for Efficient Edge Inference](https://proceedings.mlsys.org/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/ea5ffdf7da91256ecd2770f9fd2dade9-Abstract-Conference.html)** was **already present** in Kernels & Compilers, so **net +0**. G0: formal MLSys 2026, originally arXiv [2511.21513](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.21513). G1/G2: IndexSoftmax replaces a floating-point softmax island after INT8 QK with a 32-entry integer LUT, sparsity-aware clipping and integer normalization; distinct from FlashAttention IO tiling and regular post-training weight quantization. G3: author Armv8 CPU results on RK3588S2/Apple M2, up to 3.7× attention acceleration vs FP16, 2× vs ordinary INT8 and up to 61% energy reduction vs FP16; approximation quality measured on language/vision tasks. **Not** an Ascend NPU kernel, bitwise-exact FP16 softmax, or vLLM online-serving E2E benchmark. G4: first-party [code and Artifact Evaluation](https://github.com/WanliZhong/IntAttention) now linked; execution **not attempted**. **KEEP_STRICT existing row enhanced**; not duplicated and not reclassified into Quantization.

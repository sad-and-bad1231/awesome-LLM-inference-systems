## Different evidence thresholds; identical honesty floor

**G0/G1/G2 are common hard gates. G3/G4/G5 are calibrated to the paper's maturity and the actual claim.** A novel arXiv systems preprint can be admitted as **KEEP_PREPRINT_EXPLORE** when it presents a concrete, falsifiable inference-engine mechanism with **at least one inspectable support anchor** (real microbenchmark, small controlled pilot, trace, transparent analytical/simulation model, or sound proof). It does **not** need an end-to-end vLLM/Ascend benchmark, public code or peer review. The catalog should give these early ideas room to grow, especially in **RSI on inference engines**, Agent Serving, Kernel Agents and emerging inference modalities.

To guard against **story-only** papers: demand a named current bottleneck, a concrete intervention in runtime/compute/memory/compiler state, at least one observable or derivation supporting it, the closest credible prior approach, and a **discriminating next experiment**. If it cannot explain what would falsify the mechanism, or merely asserts a big future speedup with no executed/proved basis, **HOLD_STORY_ONLY / WATCH_FRONTIER**. Never treat a projected/simulated result as measured GPU/NPU throughput. No forced quotas or acceptance-ratio target.

A top-conference work can instead be labeled **KEEP_REFERENCE** when its complete evaluation teaches robust systems methodology (matched baselines, ablations, realistic workloads, quality/consistency, trade-offs); **conference acceptance is supporting context, not a quality certificate**. Do not wait for future conference announcements to admit credible arXiv work. **KEEP_PREPRINT_EVIDENCED** denotes a preprint whose primary-source experiments already support substantial scoped systems claims. Upgrade labels as evidence improves **in the same paper row**, rather than adding duplicates. See [CURATION.md](CURATION.md#two-speed-publication-review-arxiv-discovery-vs-conference-references).

## Non-negotiable audit gates

| Gate | Question | What passes | What fails / stays on hold |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G0 Identity** | Is this one real, correctly cited work? | **For preprints:** stable arXiv ID + authors + title + accessible full manuscript/version; **for published claims:** publisher DOI/official proceedings and correct venue/track. Check aliases and canonical row. | Unresolvable author/title/ID/paper alias → HOLD_IDENTITY. A non-material **date discrepancy** is a labeled metadata warning, not automatically a false paper; never invent or silently resolve it. |
| **G1 Inference scope** | Does it change executed LLM inference or a genuinely foundational dependency? | A specific, inspectable effect on KV, attention, kernels, decoding, P/D, batching, MoE, serving, distributed execution, reliability or production diagnosis | Training-only, generic agent memory, unrelated cloud/security, model-only quality change; adjacent GPU/DiT/VLM needs explicit exceptional rationale |
| **G2 Distinctive mechanism** | What is the technical abstraction and why do strong existing methods fail? | State the bottleneck, decisive implementation, and **closest prior/strong baseline**, with material difference | Same heuristic relabeled, tiny parameter change, no identifiable system novelty → REMOVE |
| **G3 Evidence validity** | Is there support **appropriate to the claim and maturity**? | **Preprint EXPLORE:** a bounded pilot/microbenchmark, trace, reproducible analytical/simulation assumption or formal argument with stated limitation and next experiment. **Preprint EVIDENCED / Conference REFERENCE:** stronger baselines, full setup, relevant TTFT/TPOT/tail/throughput/quality and ablations as applicable. | Zero inspectable technical anchor, unverifiable invented numbers, simulated/microbench speed advertised as end-to-end hardware Serving gain, ignored quality/semantic changes → HOLD_STORY_ONLY / HOLD_EVIDENCE. |
| **G4 Traceability & reproduction** | Can a reader audit the **specific support**? | Preprint: source's method, assumptions, experiment context and version; artifact link/commit **when available**. Mature/reference: prefer frozen environment, workload and code. Mark `not_attempted` versus independent replication. | No way to inspect even the claimed pilot/model/proof or contradictory evidence without explanation → HOLD. **No public code or local NPU run alone is not an arXiv exclusion.** |
| **G5 Editorial value** | Why should a systems reader spend time? | **Preprint:** new falsifiable execution question/mechanism, early negative evidence, frontier importance, useful next experiment. **Conference:** durable system abstraction, solid methods, credible evidence and research direction convergence. | Generic LLM-agent packaging, no novel execution consequence, untestable marketing, or redundant crowded variants → HOLD/REMOVE regardless of venue. |

### Review decision codes

- **KEEP_PREPRINT_EXPLORE:** credible primary arXiv identity; novel testable inference-system mechanism; **one limited but inspectable technical anchor** (including formal analysis, transparent simulation or small real benchmark); clearly scoped claims, limitations and decisive next experiment. **Not a proven serving optimization**.
- **KEEP_PREPRINT_EVIDENCED:** preprint with sufficient primary-source experiments to support its *scoped* performance/correctness claim; strong baseline and ablation where applicable, not necessarily production end-to-end.
- **KEEP_REFERENCE:** conference/systems-exemplar work with reviewed mechanisms, meaningful matched controls, workload/quality/cost boundaries and reusable methodology; **peer review alone is insufficient**.
- **HOLD_STORY_ONLY / WATCH_FRONTIER:** interesting idea but no falsifiable execution contract or inspectable evidence; preserve link/next test without pretending it is a validated canonical systems result.
- **KEEP_STRICT:** G0–G3 and G5 pass; note G4 outcome and any non-reproduction. May remain without public code if historically foundational and well evidenced.
- **KEEP_FOUNDATIONAL / KEEP_ADJACENT:** rare exceptions with **explicit** reason and honest scope labeling, never silently presented as text-LLM serving speedup.
- **HOLD_IDENTITY / HOLD_EVIDENCE:** genuinely unresolved author/paper identity, material contradictory claims, unavailable evidence for a claimed measured result. **Do not treat lack of conference acceptance, public source code, large-scale E2E numbers or local reproduction alone as HOLD for an exploratory preprint.** Keep unresolved records in review receipts or Frontier WATCH.
- **REMOVE_SCOPE / REMOVE_INCREMENTAL / REMOVE_EVIDENCE / DUPLICATE_ALIAS:** delete the canonical row (or retain only the correct original), preserve the reason and primary source in review receipts.

## Evidence receipt — staged to reduce arXiv intake latency

**Minimal arXiv EXPLORE record (one concise catalog row + compact review note):** arXiv ID/title/authors/version, executable inference problem and distinct mechanism, nearest relevant prior system, **what evidence actually exists** (pilot/trace/simulation/formal), critical model/hardware/quality or assumptions if applicable, one result or analytical claim with its proper denominator, **what is not shown**, one decisive follow-up experiment, maturity label and `not_attempted` reproduction status. Do not demand three fully tested baselines, an exhaustive artifact manifest, or a complete End-to-end Serving suite for a young field.

**Stronger EVIDENCED / REFERENCE record:** add publisher/venue/track when genuine, artifact link/commit if public, closest **three** systems (related, **not necessarily all tested**), exact model/tokens/workload/hardware/toolchain, matched comparisons, uncertainty, quality/exactness regressions, simulated versus physical measurements, and environment/dependency constraints. Preserve `not_attempted`, `artifact_inspected`, `run_failed`, `replicated`, or `not_possible`. Every quoted result remains **author-reported** unless independently reproduced.


Do not create a machine-generated JSONL/CSV source of truth in this repository. Use small GitHub PR diffs for accepted/deleted Markdown rows, plus audit notes in the PR discussion; keep any full record-level working ledger outside the published repo.

## Order of work

1. **R0 — rapid arXiv discovery and G0–G2 filter.** Scan recent sources and emerging system/agent/kernel topics frequently; accept distinct, falsifiable new mechanisms with an inspectable early technical anchor as **KEEP_PREPRINT_EXPLORE**, without requiring full Serving benchmarks or publisher acceptance. Verify title/ID/author; HOLD story-only or duplicate work. A larger discovery PR can contain concise per-paper evidence rows, but should be reviewable by topic groups.
2. **R1 — confidence upgrades / reference deep review (typically 10–20 full-method cases per review batch).** Prioritize high-value or contested preprints and influential conference papers for full evaluation, measured-vs-simulated, matched prior art, quality contract and artifact checks. Do not treat the 10–20 deep-audit recommendation as a quota on new arXiv discovery; stale historical category counts are not a review priority.
3. **R2 — reproducibility probes (only highest-value survivors).** Prefer existing vLLM/SGLang/Ascend paths when technically applicable; verify artifact commit/model/hardware/driver, run correctness first, then performance. A paper's CUDA artifact is not presumed Ascend-compatible.
4. **R3 — editorial pruning and freeze.** For each batch: PR with precise additions/deletions, summary of KEEP/HOLD/REMOVE/aliases, synchronized eight topic headings/index/homepage count, no duplicate normalized titles or publication URLs; reviewer approval then merge.

### Strong baseline checklist

Fair versions, identical model/tokenizer/checkpoint, context distribution, output lengths, batch/concurrency, precision, quality requirements, budget and hardware. When needed: modern vLLM/SGLang, FlashAttention/FlashInfer, static/continuous batching, prefix-cache policies, optimized speculative drafter or standard MoE expert placement. Report *both* throughput and SLO-correct goodput; TTFT/TPOT (including tail), memory/energy and approximation error when relevant. Never infer one metric from another.

### Hardware and model-change labels

**REAL_GPU/NPU/CPU/FPGA** (specific board), **RTL_EMU**, **PERF_SIM**, **TRACE_SIM**, **ANALYTIC_MODEL**, **AUTHOR_ONLY_CLAIM**; apply more than one label if needed, and attribute exactly what was physically tested. Likewise: **LOSSLESS_OUTPUT**, **APPROXIMATE_QUALITY_CHECKED**, **REQUIRES_FINETUNING**, **MODEL_CHANGED**. This prevents simulator, distillation or expert re-routing effects being advertised as drop-in speedups.
